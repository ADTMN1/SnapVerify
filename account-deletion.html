SnapVerify Account Deletion

SnapVerify users can request deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to natichangeagain@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: SnapVerify Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address registered with SnapVerify.
  4. We will verify your request and contact you if necessary.

What data is deleted?

We will delete the account and associated personal data that we are not legally required to retain.

Data retention

Certain records may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reasons. Any applicable retention period will be communicated during the deletion process.
