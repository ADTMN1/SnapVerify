SnapVerify Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SnapVerify ("SnapVerify," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the SnapVerify mobile application and related services (the "Service"). SnapVerify helps businesses and their authorized staff review and verify payment evidence.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and your role in a business account, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal and account information: name, email address, user or account identifiers, phone number, address when provided, and other profile or business information you submit.
  • Financial and payment information: transaction amount, currency, transaction reference, transaction date, payer and receiver details, bank or payment-account information entered for verification, subscription plan and purchase-status information.
  • Photos and receipt information: receipt or payment-evidence images that you choose to capture or upload, and text or details extracted from those images to support verification.
  • Messages: email addresses and message content used for verification codes and account-related emails, and phone numbers and message content used for staff invitation messages.
  • App activity and diagnostics: actions taken in the Service, verification and audit history, and technical information such as IP address, device or browser information, and diagnostic or security records.

Information may be provided directly by you, by an organization that manages your business account, or generated when you use the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • Creating, authenticating, and managing user, business, branch, and staff accounts.
  • Providing payment-evidence submission, review, and verification features.
  • Processing receipt images and relevant transaction details to support verification.
  • Sending one-time passwords, account notices, and staff invitations.
  • Maintaining verification records, reports, and audit trails.
  • Protecting the Service, detecting suspicious activity, preventing fraud, and enforcing our terms.
  • Providing customer support and meeting applicable legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the Service, provide requested features, or meet legal obligations. Depending on the feature used, recipients may include:

  • Check.et: relevant receipt images, extracted receipt information, and transaction or bank details may be sent to Check.et to support payment verification.
  • Email service providers: email addresses and message content may be processed by our email delivery provider(s) to send verification codes and account-related emails.
  • SMS service providers: phone numbers and message content may be processed by SMS provider(s) to deliver staff invitations and related messages.
  • Business account administrators: authorized administrators and staff may access information associated with their business, branches, roles, and verification activity according to their permissions.
  • Legal and safety recipients: we may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or the Service.

Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their handling of information is also subject to the agreements and settings applicable to the relevant integration.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain business and verification records, resolve disputes, protect the Service, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the business account's requirements.

5. Account and Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your SnapVerify account and associated personal information by emailing natichangeagain@gmail.com with the subject SnapVerify Account Deletion Request. Include the email address registered to your SnapVerify account so that we can identify the account and verify the request.

We may retain certain information where necessary to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, maintain security, or preserve legitimate business records. Information held by a business account or a third-party service may also be subject to that party's retention practices.

For more information, visit our Account Deletion page.

6. Your Choices and Responsibilities

You can choose whether to submit optional profile details and whether to upload receipt or payment-evidence images for a verification request. Some information, such as an email address and account identifier, is necessary to create and use an account. If you are using SnapVerify through a business, your business administrator may manage access and account information associated with that organization.

7. Children's Privacy

SnapVerify is a business payment-verification service and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Third-Party Services and External Links

The Service may connect to or rely on third-party services, including Check.et and communication delivery providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review those policies when using the relevant services.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service, our data practices, or applicable requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we publish a revised policy. Continued use of the Service after an update means the updated policy applies to subsequent use, subject to applicable law.
